---
name: Position rotation policy
description: Actual-play history and conservative fairness scope approved by the club.
---

Position-rotation history follows each player's last three actual appearances, not Saturday sessions. Missing weeks must not reduce their fairness priority.

**Why:** The club often plays several consecutive matches with the same roster, but returning flexible players must retain recognition of earlier lower-preference assignments.

**How to apply:** Use immutable saved preference ranks, preserve actual appearances through score/ELO corrections, and exclude explicit never-played games. Unknown legacy play state or preference rank requires confirmation, not inference.

Rotation is a final conservative tie-break, not permission to weaken formation, existing specialist safeguards, or ELO balance.

**Why:** The approved priority order places all those requirements ahead of fairness. Some rosters cannot offer a better position without violating a higher-priority objective.

**How to apply:** Keep the existing Filled-role shortage exception and manual assignment behavior. Do not widen fairness to stronger trade-offs without approval.
