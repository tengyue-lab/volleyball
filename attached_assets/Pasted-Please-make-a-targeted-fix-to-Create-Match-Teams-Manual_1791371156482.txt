# 1. Fix Swap behavior after Fill

Current behavior:

In Manual Selection, I can manually place players into Team A and Team B.

When I click **Fill**, the app assigns roles to the players correctly.

This part is working and should remain unchanged.

However, after Fill has assigned roles, if I click Swap and swap players, all of the assigned roles reset back to:

**Pending Role**

This is incorrect.

## Desired behavior

Once **Fill** has assigned roles to the players, those assigned roles should remain intact when players are swapped.

After roles have been assigned, the manual Swap functionality should behave the same way as the existing swap functionality used after **Generate Balanced Teams / Auto Balance**.

Specifically:

**A player with an assigned role may only swap with a player on the opposite team who has the SAME assigned role.**

Example:

Team A:
- John — Setter
- Alex — Outside
- Ben — Middle

Team B:
- Chris — Setter
- David — Outside
- Sam — Middle

If I click Swap on:

**John — Setter**

the only valid swap option should be:

**Chris — Setter**

The swap icons/options for David and Sam should not be shown as valid options.

John ↔ Chris should swap teams while BOTH remain Setter.

The other players and their roles should remain completely unchanged.

## Same-role swap examples

Setter ↔ Setter
= Allowed

Outside ↔ Outside
= Allowed

Middle ↔ Middle
= Allowed

Opposite ↔ Opposite
= Allowed

Libero ↔ Libero
= Allowed

Setter ↔ Outside
= Not allowed

Middle ↔ Opposite
= Not allowed

etc.

The UI should hide or disable invalid swap options in the same way the existing Auto Balance team swap functionality handles same-role swaps.

If possible, reuse the same role-compatibility logic rather than creating two different definitions of a valid role swap.

## Important distinction

Before roles have been assigned and players are still:

**Pending Role**

the existing manual swap behavior can remain.

But once players have assigned roles — whether assigned manually or through **Fill** — swapping must respect those assigned roles.

Do NOT treat a role assigned by Fill as temporary information that gets discarded during a swap.

A role assigned by Fill should be preserved during team swaps just like any other assigned role.

Most importantly:

**Swapping two players must NEVER cause unrelated players' roles to reset to Pending Role.**

A swap should only exchange the two players between Team A and Team B while preserving their assigned roles and all other role assignments.
