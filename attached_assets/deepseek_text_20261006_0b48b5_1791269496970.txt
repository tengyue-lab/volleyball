The plan says impossible pools should show an error instead of 
Filled assignments. I actually want the opposite. If a required 
position cannot be filled by a player who selected it, the app 
should still create the match, assign the best available player 
by ELO, highlight that assignment as "Filled" (orange badge), 
and show a warning banner listing which positions were short.

Only block the match if the pool is so small that a valid team 
composition is impossible (e.g., fewer than 12 players, or no 
Setter at all across the whole pool).

ADDITIONALLY — PRIORITY FOR FILLED PLAYERS IN THE NEXT MATCH

When a player is assigned to a "Filled" position (a role they did 
not select in their ranked preferences), record this in the database 
as a "filled assignment" for that player and that match.

In the NEXT match the player participates in, the matchmaking 
algorithm must give that player priority for one of their 
preferred positions. Specifically:

1. Track a flag or counter per player, e.g. "pending_priority" 
   that is set to true when they play a Filled role, and cleared 
   once they are given a Preferred role in a later match.

2. When building the next match, if a player has pending_priority 
   = true, the algorithm should treat their top-ranked preference 
   as if it were one rank higher (or apply a priority bonus in the 
   scoring system) so they are more likely to receive a role they 
   actually selected.

3. Priority only applies to positional assignment. It must NOT 
   override:
   - Validity (they still must be assigned a role from their list)
   - Position feasibility (teams must still meet the required 
     composition)
   - ELO balancing (ELO is still used after positions are assigned)

4. If two players both have pending_priority and compete for the 
   same preferred role, the tiebreaker is ELO, then the player 
   who has been waiting longer (older filled assignment).

5. Once a player receives a Preferred role, clear their 
   pending_priority flag.

6. Display this on the player profile: a small indicator showing 
   "Priority: Preferred role next match" when the flag is active, 
   so the player and admin can see it.

Add tests for this behavior:
- A player who played a Filled role last match gets their top 
  preference in the next match when possible.
- A player with pending_priority does not break team composition 
  or ELO balance.
- The flag clears correctly after a Preferred assignment.
- Two priority players competing for the same role resolve 
  deterministically (ELO, then wait time).
