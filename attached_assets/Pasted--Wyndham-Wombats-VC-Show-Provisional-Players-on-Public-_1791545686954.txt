# Wyndham Wombats VC — Show Provisional Players on Public Leaderboard

Update the existing public leaderboard so players who are still completing placement games can appear in the rankings.

## Requirements

1. Include players who have enabled **Show on Public Leaderboard**, even if their rating is still provisional or they are completing placement games.

2. Rank provisional and established players together using the existing ELO ranking and tie-breaking logic. Do not place provisional players automatically at the bottom or give them a separate ranking.

3. Show a small, subtle **Provisional** badge with a clock or similar icon beside the player's name while they have not completed the existing placement-game requirement.

4. Automatically remove the badge once the player satisfies the existing provisional/placement threshold. Reuse the current backend logic for this status; do not invent a new threshold.

5. Preserve the existing leaderboard layout, Top 5 limit if applicable, tier displays, and mobile responsiveness.

6. Do not expose numeric ELO ratings, rating changes, or other admin-only information to the public.

7. Players who have disabled public leaderboard visibility must remain completely excluded, regardless of provisional status.

8. Use the existing backend privacy-safe leaderboard response. Ensure the backend includes eligible provisional players rather than merely removing a frontend filter. Do not bypass the visibility preference.

9. If a player has zero completed games but has opted into the public leaderboard, follow the existing eligibility rules for unplayed accounts; do not silently treat them as an established player. Explain any ambiguity before changing zero-game eligibility.

## Testing

Verify:
- Provisional player opted in → appears with rank and Provisional badge.
- Established player opted in → appears with rank and no badge.
- Any player opted out → hidden.
- Provisional player can appear in the Top 5.
- Completing placement games automatically removes the badge.
- Existing ranking order remains unchanged for the same ELO values.
- No private ELO data is exposed.

## Scope

Keep this change focused on leaderboard eligibility and presentation.

Do not change ELO formulas, K factors, placement-game thresholds, player ratings, tier thresholds, match history, or position-rotation fairness.

Do not reset data, add database columns unless strictly necessary, or deploy automatically.

After implementation, report the files changed and test results.
