CHANGE 1 — ELO PUBLIC / PRIVATE TOGGLE

ELO rating is publicly visible.

Requirements:
- Add a boolean field to the player data model, e.g. 
  "elo_public" (default: true, so existing players stay visible 
  unless they change it).
- On the player edit screen, add a toggle labeled something like 
  "Show my ELO on the public leaderboard."
- The player can switch this on or off at any time.
- When off:
  - The player does not appear on the public leaderboard.
  - Other players cannot see this player's ELO on any public-facing 
    screen (leaderboard, match history, player list, etc.).
  - The admin (you, when logged in) can still see the ELO on the 
    admin/edit screens so matchmaking and ELO updates continue to work.
- The player's own profile should always show their own ELO to 
  themselves, even when the toggle is off.
- ELO calculation, matchmaking, and match result updates must 
  continue to use the player's real ELO regardless of this setting. 
  The toggle only affects visibility, not the rating itself.
