## BUG 1 — Swapping incorrectly assigns roles to everyone

Current behavior:

When I manually swap two players between Team A and Team B, players who previously showed:

**Pending Role**

suddenly get assigned positions/roles.

This should NOT happen.

A manual player swap must NOT trigger automatic role assignment, position filling, matchmaking, or team balancing.

### Correct behavior

If a player is:

**Pending Role**

before the swap, they should remain:

**Pending Role**

after the swap.

Only players whose roles were explicitly locked by the user should retain a role.

Example before swap:

Team A:
- John — Pending Role
- Alex — Setter 🔒
- Ben — Pending Role

Team B:
- Chris — Pending Role
- David — Setter 🔒
- Sam — Pending Role

If John and Chris are swapped:

Team A:
- Chris — Pending Role
- Alex — Setter 🔒
- Ben — Pending Role

Team B:
- John — Pending Role
- David — Setter 🔒
- Sam — Pending Role

Do NOT automatically assign positions to John, Chris, Ben, or Sam.

A manual swap should ONLY exchange the two players' team membership.

Existing explicit role locks must remain unchanged.

## BUG 2 — Swapping should work before all selected players are assigned to teams

Current behavior:

Swap Player does not work until every selected player has been moved from the available/selected player list into Team A or Team B.

For example:

Selected player pool contains multiple players.

Currently assigned:

Team A:
- 2 players

Team B:
- 3 players

Other selected players are still waiting to be manually assigned.

At this point I should STILL be able to swap one of the 2 Team A players with one of the 3 Team B players.

The existence of unassigned players should NOT disable Swap Player.

### Correct behavior

Swap availability should depend only on:

1. The player being swapped.
2. Players currently assigned to the opposite team.
3. The existing locked-role compatibility rules.

It should NOT depend on whether all selected players have already been assigned to Team A or Team B.

If Team A has at least one player and Team B has at least one valid swap candidate, swapping should work.

Players still sitting in the available/unassigned player pool should simply remain there and should not affect the swap.

---

## Preserve the existing swap rules

Keep the rules implemented previously:

### Unlocked / Pending Role player

A player without a locked role can swap with valid players on the opposite team according to the existing swap rules.

### Locked player

A player with a locked role can only swap with an opposite-team player with the SAME locked role.

Example:

Setter 🔒 ↔ Setter 🔒
= allowed

Setter 🔒 ↔ Outside 🔒
= not allowed

Setter 🔒 ↔ Pending Role
= not allowed

Do not change these rules as part of this bug fix.

## Important state rule

Please inspect the swap handler carefully.

A manual swap should essentially change:

**Player A: Team A → Team B**

and

**Player B: Team B → Team A**

while preserving the rest of their existing manual-selection state.

It should NOT call or reuse a function that:

- automatically assigns roles
- fills positions
- balances teams
- regenerates teams
- resets manual team state
- clears the selected player pool
- requires the unassigned-player pool to be empty

If the current swap handler is reusing one of those functions, separate the swap operation from that automatic logic.
