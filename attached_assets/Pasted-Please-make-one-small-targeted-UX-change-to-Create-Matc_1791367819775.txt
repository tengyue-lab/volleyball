Please make one small targeted UX change to **Create Match → Step 3: Teams**.

Do not change the matchmaking/balancing algorithm, player-selection logic, ELO calculations, or unrelated UI.

## 1. Rename "Auto Balance Teams"

In the Teams step, rename the button:

**Auto Balance Teams**

to:

**Generate Balanced Teams**

This should use exactly the same existing balancing functionality. This is only a label/UI change.

Do NOT modify how the balancing algorithm works.

## 2. Fix "Clear Selection" in the Teams step

There is currently a **Clear Selection** action in the Teams step.

Right now, this appears to clear the selected player pool as well, which forces the user to go back to the Players step and select all participating players again.

Change this behavior.

When **Clear Selection** is clicked from the Teams step:

- Clear all current assignments from **Team A and Team B**.
- Keep the full selected player pool intact.
- Do NOT deselect/remove the players chosen in Step 2: Players.
- The same selected players should immediately become available again for team assignment.
- The user should remain on the **Teams** step.
- The user should be able to manually rebuild the teams or click **Generate Balanced Teams** again without returning to the Players step.

In other words, there are now two different concepts:

**Players step → Clear Selection**
- Clears the selected match roster/player pool.
- Existing behavior can remain.

**Teams step → Clear Selection**
- Clears ONLY Team A / Team B assignments.
- Does NOT clear the selected match roster/player pool.

Please make sure these two actions are handled separately and do not accidentally share a reset function that clears the entire match session.

## Expected flow

Example:

I select 14 players in the **Players** step.

I continue to **Teams**.

I click **Generate Balanced Teams**.

The 14 players are assigned between Team A and Team B.

I click **Clear Selection**.

Expected result:

- Team A becomes empty.
- Team B becomes empty.
- All 14 selected players remain part of the match.
- All 14 are available for team assignment again.
- I stay on the Teams step.
- I can click Generate Balanced Teams again or manually assign teams.

I should NOT have to return to Players and select those 14 players again.

Please make the smallest targeted change necessary.

Run the relevant tests/build after making the change and do not deploy.
